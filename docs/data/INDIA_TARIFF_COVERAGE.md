## Known sourcing gaps and collision risks

- **Kerala vs. Karnataka regulator codes.** Kerala is coded `KSERC` (not
  `KERC`) specifically to avoid collision with Karnataka Electricity
  Regulatory Commission's `KERC`.
- **Karnataka vs. West Bengal licensee codes.** Karnataka's Mysuru-area
  discom (Chamundeshwari Electricity Supply Corporation) is coded `CESCK`
  (not `CESC`) to avoid collision with West Bengal's CESC Limited (Kolkata).
- **Uttar Pradesh discom privatization.** PuVVNL and DVVNL's current
  ownership status (state-owned vs. privatized) is unconfirmed as of
  registry seeding — UP has pursued privatization of these two discoms over
  several years with contested/stalled processes.
- **Odisha discom ownership history.** Originally state-owned, then
  partially privatized to Reliance-linked entities, then that arrangement
  broke down with administrators appointed (~2020), then Tata Power took
  over across all four circles (~2021). Current operator names
  (TPCODL/TPWODL/TPSODL/TPNODL) are seeded at medium confidence, pending
  verification against OERC.
- **Andhra Pradesh post-bifurcation discom count.** Seeded with three
  discoms (APSPDCL/APEPDCL/APCPDCL); some sources describe only two
  consolidated discoms post-2014. `APCPDCL`'s continued existence needs
  verification against APERC.
- **Small states/UTs regulatory arrangement.** For Mizoram, Nagaland,
  Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh, the Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep,
  Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and Chandigarh, it is genuinely unconfirmed
  (in this registry seeding pass) whether a standalone SERC exists, whether
  a JERC covers them, or whether distribution is run directly by a
  government department rather than a corporatized licensee. These are
  marked `BLOCKED` rather than guessed.

## Source registry status

Only 5 authoritative sources are configured (Forum of Regulators directory,
GERC tariff schedules, KERC tariff orders, DERC tariff orders, MSEDCL tariff
details), inherited from the pre-existing registry and not yet expanded to
match the 18 seeded regulators / 51 seeded licensees. **Expanding
`sources.yaml` to cover the priority-18 jurisdictions' regulators and major
licensees, and running a live verification pass against each, is the largest
remaining gap** before any jurisdiction can move from `IN_PROGRESS` to
`COVERED`.
